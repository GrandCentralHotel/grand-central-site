How to use this document

Sit with the owner and work through one section at a time. “I don’t know” is a useful answer—it identifies what must be checked with staff, suppliers, records or the booking platforms.

Security: Do not write passwords, banking details, alarm codes, guest information or ID numbers here. Record who controls each account, then place credentials in an approved password manager.

Suggested order

  1. Complete Sections 1–3 first so the OTA photographs can be changed.
  2. Confirm every room fact before changing public descriptions.
  3. Ask the general manager, reception and housekeeping to verify operational answers.
  4. Mark every unresolved item with an owner and deadline in the action register.
