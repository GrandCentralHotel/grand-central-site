Spaces with a story to tell

Heritage halls, practical studios and quieter rooms for events and creative work

Make an enquiry

Choose the space that fits the occasion

Grand Central Hotel's former public halls and waiting rooms now support exhibitions, celebrations, filming, photography and collaborative work.

Each space has a different scale and atmosphere. Arrange a viewing with the venue team to discuss access, layout and the practical needs of your project.

The spaces

01 · Former waiting hall

Grand Hall

The building's most monumental room, with cathedral-height ceilings, arched windows, terrazzo floors and strong natural light.

  • Ceremonies & receptions
  • Concerts & cultural events
  • Exhibitions, filming & editorial shoots
View details →

02 · Arrival space

Grand Foyer

A bright, flexible concourse where the station's steelwork and long windows create a distinctive welcome.

  • Guest arrival & registration
  • Receptions & exhibition displays
  • Photography & creative showcases
View details →

03 · Production space

Photography Studio

A flexible studio supporting portraits, fashion editorials and creative campaigns, with tall ceilings and adaptable working arrangements.

  • Portraits & product work
  • Fashion & commercial campaigns
  • Small filming productions
View details →

04 · Former Ladies' Waiting Room

Meeting Room

A quieter timber-panelled room with original detailing, suited to focused gatherings away from the larger halls.

  • Meetings & presentations
  • Workshops & planning sessions
  • Client consultations
View details →

05 · Former dining room

Waiting Room

A characterful heritage room for smaller occasions, with a coffered ceiling, warm chandeliers and an open central floor.

  • Private functions
  • Workshops & community gatherings
  • Displays & smaller events
View details →

06 · Creative workspace

Art Share Space

Flexible rooms for independent creatives who need an inspiring place to work, meet clients or collaborate with a small team.

  • Studio & project work
  • Workshops & shared practice
  • Creative meetings & small offices
View details →

Practical support for creative plans

The venue team can help shape the spaces around each project and confirm the practical arrangements during enquiry.

01

Filming & photography permissions

Discuss location access, timing and the spaces required for commercial, editorial or screen production.

02

Exhibition equipment

Modular panels, plinths, pedestals and display blocks are available for events in the building or for external hire.

03

Studio & creative workspace

Ask about short-term studio arrangements, collaborative rooms and practical facilities for independent work.

04

Venue tours & planning

View the spaces with the team before confirming the most suitable room and event flow.
