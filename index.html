The station's original arrivals and departures board stood in the concourse, under the sign for platforms three to seven.

The ticket hall is still the front desk

Guests check in under the original coffered ceiling, at the counter where tickets were once sold, beneath a clock set into amber glass. The terrazzo floor and its medallion are the ones laid when the building opened.

Very little of this is reproduction. It is the reason people photograph the lobby before they get to their room, and the reason a night here is not the same as a night in a tower on Queen Street.

Find the entrance

Same doors, different century

Passengers once read the board here under a vaulted steel roof, on their way to Papakura, Henderson and Helensville. The arches, the clock and the floor are still where they were.

Four ways to sleep in a railway building

Upstairs is calm and unfussy: tall sash windows, good beds, bathrooms that work. From a pod bunk with your own curtain, through to a twin with room for two and their luggage.

Saffron is still serving

The restaurant downstairs does Indian-Western fusion and takes walk-ins as well as guests. Useful when you get back from the arena and Quay Street has shut for the night.

Inside Grand Central

Auckland railway heritage

The ticket hall is still the front desk

Built from 1928 to 1930 as Auckland's grand gateway, the former railway station was designed to make an impression. Guests now check in where tickets were once sold, beneath the original coffered ceiling and clock.

This is not a railway-themed hotel. It is the railway station itself—adapted for a new journey while much of its character remains.

Getting here

Arrive through the old station

Grand Central is inside Auckland's former railway station at 26 Te Taou Crescent, beside Spark Arena. Use the full street address when booking a taxi or rideshare so you arrive at the hotel rather than Auckland's current train station.

Arrival address

26 Te Taou Crescent
Auckland CBD 1010

Te Taou Crescent is reached from Beach Road. On-site parking is limited, so please call ahead if you need a space.

Open directions ↗
  1. 01

    Find the red-brick station

    Follow Te Taou Crescent to number 26. The hotel occupies the large heritage railway building beside Spark Arena.

  2. 02

    Enter through the heritage lobbies

    Use the building entrance leading into the Grand and Lesser Lobbies. You are entering the original public spaces of the former station.

  3. 03

    Continue to the former ticket hall

    Once inside, follow the Grand Central signs to the former ticket hall. The hotel reception and 24-hour front desk are located there.

Before you arrive

Frequently asked questions

How close is Grand Central to Spark Arena?

The hotel is approximately a three-minute walk from Spark Arena, making it an easy base for concerts, sport and live events.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes. On-site parking is available, subject to availability. We recommend calling the front desk on 09 365 1428 before arrival to reserve a space and confirm the current rate.

Does Grand Central have free Wi-Fi?

Yes. Complimentary fibre Wi-Fi is available throughout the property, including in guest rooms.

Can I arrive late?

Yes. The front desk is staffed 24 hours, so the team can assist with late arrivals. Let the hotel know in advance if you expect to arrive late.

Can the hotel store my luggage?

Yes. Luggage storage is available for guests before check-in and after check-out.

Are pets allowed?

No. Pets cannot be accommodated in the hotel’s guest rooms.

Is there somewhere to eat on site?

Yes. Saffron is located downstairs in the Grand Hall and welcomes hotel guests and walk-ins.
