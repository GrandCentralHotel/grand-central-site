The ticket hall is still the front desk

Guests check in under the original coffered ceiling, at the counter where tickets were once sold, beneath a clock set into amber glass. The terrazzo floor and its medallion are the ones laid when the building opened.

Very little of this is reproduction. It is the reason people photograph the lobby before they get to their room, and the reason a night here is not the same as a night in a tower on Queen Street.

Find the entrance

Same doors, different century

Passengers once read the board here under a vaulted steel roof, on their way to Papakura, Henderson and Helensville. The arches, the clock and the floor are still where they were.

Saffron is still serving

Saffron is an independently operated South Indian restaurant downstairs. It is closed on Mondays and open Tuesday to Sunday, 11 a.m.–3 p.m. and 5:30–9 p.m. Saffron may stay open later when there are events at Spark Arena. Breakfast is not currently available or included with any room.

Getting here

Arrive through the old station

Grand Central Hotel is inside Auckland's former railway station at 26 Te Taou Crescent, beside Spark Arena. Use the full street address when booking a taxi or rideshare so you arrive at the hotel rather than Auckland's current train station.

Entrance, parking and directions

Te Taou Crescent is reached from Beach Road. On-site parking costs NZ$15 overnight and is subject to availability. Request a space when booking or ask reception on arrival.

    Enter through the heritage lobbies

Before you arrive

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2 p.m. and check-out is by 10 a.m. Reception is open from 9 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. Please contact reception in advance to arrange an arrival outside these hours.

Can I check in early or check out late?

Early check-in costs NZ$30 before noon, or NZ$20 between noon and 2 p.m. Late check-out between 10 a.m. and noon costs NZ$20. Please contact reception to confirm availability and arrange either option.

What do I need for check-in and payment?

Please bring identification for check-in. Depending on your booking terms, payment may be taken when you book; if it has not been taken, you can pay at the hotel. Most major payment cards are accepted. Contact reception if you need to check a particular payment method.

The hotel normally places a credit-card pre-authorisation hold of NZ$50–NZ$100. This is a temporary hold, rather than a payment. Ask reception to confirm the amount for your stay.

Room prices displayed during booking include taxes and fees. Optional services such as parking, laundry, early check-in and late check-out cost extra at the rates listed here.

What is the minimum check-in age?

The normal minimum check-in age is 18. Guests aged 16 or 17 must contact the hotel and arrange parental authorisation before making a booking.

Is housekeeping included during my stay?

Daily housekeeping is automatic and includes emptying bins, replacing towels, making beds and cleaning the bathroom. Please use the “Do not disturb” sign or let the team know if you do not want service.

For very long stays, the housekeeping schedule is agreed with you separately and may be weekly.

Can I check in online?

Online check-in is not currently available. Please check in at reception, which is open from 9 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. If you expect to arrive outside these hours, contact reception in advance to arrange your arrival.

Is there a guest laundry?

Yes. The on-site guest laundry costs NZ$3 per wash and NZ$3 per dry.

How close is Grand Central Hotel to Spark Arena?

The hotel is approximately a three-minute walk from Spark Arena, making it an easy base for concerts, sport and live events.

Is parking available at the hotel?

On-site parking costs NZ$15 overnight. Request parking during booking or ask reception on arrival; spaces are subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed. Some parking areas have a 2.2-metre height limit, while others have no height restriction. Contact reception before arrival if you need a space for a taller vehicle.

Does Grand Central Hotel have free Wi-Fi?

Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the property, including in guest rooms.

Can I arrive late?

Reception is open from 9 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. If you expect to arrive outside these hours, please contact reception in advance to arrange your arrival.

Can the hotel store my luggage?

Yes. Luggage storage is free for guests before check-in and after check-out.

Are pets allowed?

No. Pets cannot be accommodated in the hotel’s guest rooms.

Is there somewhere to eat on site?

Yes. Saffron is an independently operated South Indian restaurant downstairs in the Grand Hall. It is closed Mondays and open Tuesday to Sunday, 11 a.m.–3 p.m. and 5:30–9 p.m. Saffron may stay open later when there are events at Spark Arena. Breakfast is not currently available or included with any room.
