Your wedding at the station

Grand Central Hotel brings ceremony, portraits and celebration together beneath one historic roof. The Grand Hall offers scale and theatre, while the Grand Foyer and surrounding rooms create quieter moments throughout the day.

On-site accommodation and Saffron restaurant make it easier for couples and guests to settle in, celebrate and stay close.
