Grand Central Hotel sits on Te Taou Crescent, within walking distance of Spark Arena and within easy reach of Britomart, the waterfront and Queen Street. Choose a Standard Double or Deluxe King for a city break, or a Deluxe Twin for friends travelling together or a small family.

Accessibility information

All guest rooms have step-free routes. Lift access is available to most guest rooms. The hotel does not have wheelchair-adapted guest rooms, accessible parking or hearing/vision facilities. One wheelchair-accessible public toilet is available in the public area. Please contact reception before booking to discuss the entrance, route to your room and whether the facilities meet your needs.
