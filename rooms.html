A practical base in Auckland CBD

Grand Central Hotel sits on Te Taou Crescent, three minutes on foot from Spark Arena and within easy reach of Britomart, the waterfront and Queen Street. Choose a Standard Double or Deluxe King for a city break, or a Deluxe Twin for friends travelling together or a small family.

Accessibility information

Lift access is available to most guest rooms, although some routes within the building include steps. The hotel does not have wheelchair-adapted guest rooms. A wheelchair-accessible public toilet is available. Please contact reception before booking to discuss the entrance, route to your room and whether the facilities meet your needs.
