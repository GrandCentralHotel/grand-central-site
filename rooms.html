A practical base in Auckland CBD

Grand Central sits on Te Taou Crescent, three minutes on foot from Spark Arena and within easy reach of Britomart, the waterfront and Queen Street. Choose a private room for a city break, a flexible twin for a small family, or a pod when the location matters more than extra space.
