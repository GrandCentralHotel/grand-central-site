Getting to Spark Arena

Start at the hotel entrance on 26 Te Taou Crescent and follow the walking route towards Mahuhu Crescent. Spark Arena’s main entrance is on Mahuhu Crescent, between Beach Road and Quay Street. Check the route and your event’s entry information before setting off.

Planning your stay

Can I walk to Spark Arena from the hotel?

Yes. The hotel is on Te Taou Crescent, within walking distance of the arena’s Mahuhu Crescent entrance. Use the walking directions to plan your route and allow time for event queues.

Which room works for friends travelling together?

The Deluxe Twin has a king bed and a king-single bed and accommodates up to three guests. Compare the Deluxe Twin with our other room options.

How do I book direct?

Select Check Availability to view dates and room options in the hotel’s official Cloudbeds booking engine. For arrival information, read our guest guide.
